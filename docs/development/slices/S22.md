# S22 — Typed expenses and receipt albums from Telegram

**Depends on:** S19 (receipts from Telegram)
**State:** done

**Amends S19:**

- **A typed line is an expense.** In a private chat, a linked member can type
  `1500 taxi to the client` or `USD 20 Figma #software`, with no receipt.
  `parseExpenseText` (core, pure) takes the amount (the number with a currency
  beside it, otherwise the only or largest), the day (`today`, `yesterday`,
  `YYYY-MM-DD`), the category (`#tag` or a category's name) and leaves the rest
  as the description; the caption's project rules apply. No amount, no expense:
  the bot says how to write one. Not read by AI. Groups stay receipts-only.
- **An album is one expense.** Telegram sends an album's photos as separate
  updates sharing a `media_group_id`. The inbox claims an album only once none
  of its photos arrived in the last `ALBUM_SETTLE_MS` (3 s, by the database's
  clock), claims it whole so the batch limit never splits it, and hands it to
  `processAlbum`. Each receipt is read once (cached on its own inbox row);
  `combineDrafts` adds the amounts, keeps each receipt's reading under
  `extraction.receipts`, and files it under the caption's category or the
  most common one read. Disagreeing categories and unreadable receipts are
  told to the member. Different currencies are separate drafts.
- **`expense.createDraft`** (`POST /expenses/drafts`, multipart or JSON):
  up to ten `files` and one `draft`, created in one transaction. Both the
  typed and the album paths use it; `expense.createFromReceipt` now shares its
  body. The API's multipart parsing keeps repeated fields as arrays.

## Definition of done

1. `pnpm vitest run --project unit packages/core/src/modules/finance/expense-from-message.test.ts` passes.
2. `pnpm vitest run --project integration packages/core/test/telegram.test.ts packages/core/test/procedures.test.ts` passes.
3. `pnpm openapi:check` passes.
4. By hand: type `1500 taxi` to the bot and confirm it; send three receipt
   photos as one album and see one draft with their total and three receipts.

## Notes

- The same photo twice inside one album is not flagged as a duplicate: the
  duplicate check looks at other expenses only.
- A photo that arrives more than the settle window after the rest of its album
  (a very slow upload) becomes its own expense.
- The web form still takes one receipt; `expense.createDraft` is there for it
  to take several.
