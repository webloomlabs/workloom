# S16 — Recurring billing: several periods on one invoice

**Depends on:** S11 (recurring billing), S14 (line periods and billing types)
**State:** done

**Amends the plan:**

- **One invoice can bill several periods.** A client on a monthly retainer who
  pays a quarter ahead gets one invoice for three months. Each period still has
  its own `billing_schedule_invoices` row, so the unique key on
  `(schedule, period_start)` still makes billing any month twice impossible;
  only the one-invoice-one-period key went. `generated_count` and
  `max_occurrences` count periods, not invoices.
- **A schedule can always bill ahead** (`periods_per_invoice`), or a person can
  raise N periods on demand (`billingSchedule.generate` with `periods`, a
  `layout`, and an optional prepay discount).
- **Deleting a generated draft no longer loses its periods.** Before, the
  cascade freed the periods but nothing moved `next_run_on` back, so they were
  never raised again. Now `invoice.delete` rewinds the schedule to the earliest
  of them (`rebill`, the default) — reopening an agreement those periods had
  finished — or leaves them unbilled and says so in the audit log (`skip`).
  The schedule then steps over any period billed out of turn.

## Scope

In:
- `billingSchedule.generate` `{ periods, layout, discountPercent }`;
  `periodsPerInvoice` on create and update; `billingScheduleLine.update`;
  `invoice.delete` `{ schedulePeriods }`; `invoice.recurring.periods`.
- Invoice lines raised by a schedule carry the billing type and period, so the
  printed invoice says "Quarterly" and "Jan – Mar 2026".
- Recurring page: bill several periods, edit a line; invoice page: keep or skip
  a deleted draft's periods.

Out:
- Sending generated invoices automatically (still a person's decision).

## Notes

- Periods billed together are stated as one range only when they are
  contiguous; otherwise the invoice gets a line per period.
- Reopening an ended schedule from a deleted draft is audited
  (`billing_schedule.rewound`) rather than emitted: `invoice.delete` declares
  only `invoice.deleted`.
