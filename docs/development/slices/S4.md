# S4 — Client view

**Depends on:** S3
**State:** done

**Amends the plan:**

- **The client view is the company page**, at `/companies/[id]`, with one tab per
  section, rather than a separate client record or URL. There is no clients table
  (see [S3](S3.md)), so a second page for the same company would be two views to
  keep in step. A new **Clients** list (`/clients`) shows current and former clients
  with their open deals and contact counts.
- **"Every MVP tab populated as its slice lands" is now a test.** Sections are
  declared in `CLIENT_SECTIONS` (`packages/core/src/modules/crm/clients.ts`), each with
  the slice that builds it. Once a slice is added to `SHIPPED_SLICES`
  (`packages/core/src/release.ts`), `clients.test.ts` fails until that section counts
  its records. `SHIPPED_SLICES` replaces the private list in the event catalogue, so
  one list drives both.
- **Contracts have no section of their own.** Spec §7 lists them; in the MVP a quote
  is the agreement, and contracts are Phase 2 in the roadmap.
- **Home opens Clients** for roles that can read companies but not deals, such as
  developers.

## Scope

In: the tabbed client view (Overview, Contacts, Deals, Activity, Details); upcoming
sections (Projects, Quotes, Invoices, Payments, Expenses) and planned ones (Support,
Maintenance, Infrastructure, Documents) shown but not openable; `company.summary`
(`GET /companies/{id}/summary`); `client.list` (`GET /clients`); the Clients page and
navigation entry.

Out: the content of upcoming sections (S5, S7a–c); Phase 2 sections; a client portal
(Phase 2).

## Found and fixed

- **In production builds, domain errors lost their messages in the UI.** Next bundles
  `@workloom/core` more than once, so a Server Action and the procedure it called could
  hold different copies of `DomainError`. `instanceof` failed across the copies, and
  "Unknown time zone" became "Something went wrong". Not-found and permission errors
  had the same problem. It never showed up under `pnpm dev`, and it depended on how
  chunks were split: S3's production run passed, and S4's new modules changed the
  split. Error classes now brand their instances with `Symbol.for` and answer
  `instanceof` from the brand. `context.test.ts` loads a second copy of the module to
  prove it.

## Notes for later slices

- **When a slice ships a client section** (S5 projects; S7a quotes; S7b invoices;
  S7c payments and expenses), do three things together:
  1. Add the slice to `SHIPPED_SLICES`.
  2. Add a counter to `SECTION_COUNTERS`.
  3. Replace that section's `null` in `SECTION_CONTENT` on the company page.

  The unit test enforces the first two. If the third is missed, the page shows a
  notice saying the section has no page yet, rather than breaking.
- **Sections are filtered by permission on the server.** A developer's summary has no
  deals, quotes, invoices, payments, or expenses at all, rather than zero counts. New
  sections need a `permission`.
- **Decided: notes logged on a lead before conversion stay hidden from roles without
  `lead:read`**, including on the client page. S3 raised the question. These are sales
  notes, often with budgets in them, in the same category as the deal notes already
  hidden from developers. The company timeline labels them "Logged while a lead" for
  those who can see them.
- **An error class that transports recognise must use the brand** in
  `packages/core/src/context.ts`. A plain `class XError extends Error` that is caught
  with `instanceof` in `apps/web` will pass in dev and fail in production.
- **`SHIPPED_SLICES` also switches on event types.** Adding a slice marks its catalogue
  events as emitted in the webhook subscription picker, so add it once the events
  really are emitted.
