# S14 — Invoice document v2

**Depends on:** S7b (invoices, PDFs), S7c (payments), S11 (document sequences)
**State:** done

**Amends the plan:**

- **A final invoice can state the whole project and take earlier invoices off
  it.** A deduction is a line (`invoice_lines.kind = 'deduction'`) pointing at an
  issued invoice of the same client and currency, built from that invoice's own
  priced lines — one negative line per tax it used — so tax nets out exactly and
  revenue is never counted twice: deposit 26,000 + final 54,000 = 80,000. An
  invoice can be taken off only one other, and cannot be cancelled while it is.
  Whether it had been paid ("Already paid" vs "Previously invoiced") is fixed on
  the line when the final invoice is issued.
- **An invoice can be due on an event.** `due_label` ("At launch") prints in
  place of a date; the invoice is issued with no due date, is never overdue,
  and is given its date once, later (`invoice.setDueDate`). The invoice guard
  allows exactly that one change after issue.
- **Numbers follow a format and can restart each year.** `document_sequences`
  gained `scope`, `format`, and `reset`; the year in a number comes from the
  document's issue date, so a backdated invoice joins last year's run. The
  count can be moved forward (an agency starting at 144), never onto a number
  already issued.
- **The printed invoice was redesigned** to the agency's own style: logo, a
  BALANCE DUE headline, line titles with a quieter detail line, a Billing
  column, statement totals ending in a coloured bar, a payment-details box,
  and a configurable footer.

## Scope

In:
- Organization: fiscal year start, logo (PNG/JPEG checked by magic bytes),
  accent colour, payment details, default notes and terms, footer, and whether
  input tax is reclaimed (used from S15).
- Invoice and quote lines: `detail` and `billing_type`; invoice lines also a
  service period.
- `invoiceLine.addDeduction`, `invoice.setDueDate`, `documentSequence.list|update`,
  `organization.uploadLogo|removeLogo|logo`.
- The invoice email states the amount due and the due label.
- Settings → Organization (branding, bank details) and Settings → Numbering;
  the invoice editor (detail, billing, period, deductions, due label, due date).

Out:
- Raising the statement invoice automatically from a project's billing plan (S18).
- Credit notes (Phase 2).

## Notes

- A document discount and a deduction cannot be on the same invoice: the
  discount would be shared across the deduction and change what the earlier
  invoice is said to have demanded. Discount the lines.
- `presentTotals` (finance/document-totals.ts) is the one place the statement
  reading is worked out; the screen, the PDF, and the email all use it.
