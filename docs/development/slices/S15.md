# S15 — Expenses v2: receipts, drafts, categories

**Depends on:** S7c (expenses), S14 (organization settings)
**State:** done

**Amends the plan:**

- **Expense receipts land here, not in Phase 2.** S7c deferred them; an
  expense with no receipt is the first thing an auditor or the tax office
  questions. They are their own table (`expense_receipts`) with a cascade from
  the expense, as S7c said they should be.
- **An expense can be a draft.** A draft is a guess — read from a receipt photo
  — and counts for nothing until a person confirms it: not in project margins
  (`project_financials_v`), the dashboard, the revenue report, or a rebill. It
  is numbered (`EXP-0042`) when confirmed. One typed in by hand is confirmed at
  once.
- **Tax on an expense is a cost unless it is reclaimed.** New
  `organization.reclaims_input_tax` (default true). An agency below the VAT
  threshold sets it false and its expenses cost what the receipt says.
- **Categories are the agency's own,** each with how the tax pack treats it:
  deductible, not deductible, or capital. Existing expenses keep their keys;
  every organization is seeded with them.
- **You may correct, evidence, and confirm your own expense** with
  `expense:create` alone; anyone else's needs `expense:update`. This is what
  lets a manager fix the draft their own receipt photo produced.

## Scope

In:
- `expense.confirm`, `expenseReceipt.upload|list|download|delete`,
  `expenseCategory.list|create|update`; `expense.create` takes `status`,
  `amountIncludesTax`, payment method, paid-on, reimbursable.
- Receipts are checked by their bytes (JPEG, PNG, WebP, GIF, HEIC, PDF), at
  most 10 MB, keyed by ids, flagged when the same file is already on another
  expense, and removed from storage with the expense.
- Images can be shown inline through a signed URL; everything else still
  downloads.
- The expense form records the receipt's total and works out the net; receipts
  attach as it is recorded; drafts and missing receipts have their own views.
- Settings → Expense categories.

Out:
- Reading receipts automatically (S19).
- Approval workflows beyond draft/confirmed.

## Notes

- `expenseCost` / `expenseCostSql` (finance/expense-cost.ts) are the one rule
  for what an expense cost; the view's `spend` CTE says the same in SQL.
