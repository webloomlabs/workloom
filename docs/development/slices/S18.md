# S18 — Quotes into projects: alternatives, accepting into the contract, and the statement final invoice

**Depends on:** S7a (quotes), S11 (recurring billing), S13 (revisions and billing stages), S14 (statements and deductions), S17 (project billing models)
**State:** done

**Amends the plan:**

- **Accepting a quote carries it into the project** (US-07). `quote.accept`
  now does in one transaction what used to be done by hand:
  - finds the project (the quote's own, one named, or a new one started from
    the quote) and links the quote to it;
  - **the first one-time price sets the contract value** and
    `projects.contract_quote_id`; a later quote is **added as an accepted
    revision**, so the contracted value stays contract + accepted revisions and
    nothing is counted twice (`applyAs` overrides: `base`, `addition`, `none`);
  - **recurring lines never join the contract.** Each interval becomes a
    billing schedule, **paused** until someone resumes it at launch
    (`billing_schedules.quote_id` records where it came from);
  - optionally sets up a billing plan: a deposit and the balance at launch, or
    two halves (`stagePreset`).
- **Quotes can be alternatives** (`quote_option_groups`). A quote offered as
  "Option B" sits in a group with its siblings; accepting one declines the
  others that were sent, and a decided group refuses to send another. A
  partial unique index allows one accepted quote per group, so two people
  accepting at once leave exactly one accepted.
- **A billing stage can be "the balance"** — whatever the contract has left
  after the other stages — and can **invoice as a statement**: the whole
  contracted scope ("Initially quoted project fee", then each "Additional
  scope"), each earlier stage invoice taken off, and the balance due. This is
  the sample invoice (Kyla & Co., WL-2026-0144), produced by the plan rather
  than typed. The release refuses unless it is the last stage, every earlier
  stage invoice is issued, and the tax rates match.
- **Quotes have a PDF and an email** (`quote.download`, `quote.email`, and an
  email option on `quote.send`), using the S14 document template; public
  document links serve quotes as well as invoices.
- **`invoice.fromQuote` needs an accepted quote**, takes its one-time lines
  only, and refuses a quote already invoiced. Accepting is now the step that
  makes a quote billable.
- `shareOf` in the billing plan uses exact rational maths instead of floats.

## Scope

In:
- `quote.accept` (extended), `quoteOptionGroup.create`, `quote.download`,
  `quote.email`; `quote.create|update` take a project and option fields;
  `quote.duplicate` can make an alternative; quote lines take a detail,
  billing type, and interval.
- Billing stages: the `balance` basis, `invoiceStyle` (`single_line` |
  `statement`), and a due label on release.
- Web: the quote page's accept card (project, contract effect, recurring
  schedules, billing plan), Download PDF, Email, "Offer an alternative", and
  the options list; a Quotes tab on the project; the balance basis and
  statement style on the billing plan.

Out:
- The client accepting through the public link.
- Optional quote lines.

## Notes

- The quote guard lets `project_id`, `option_group_id`, and `option_label` go
  from empty to a value once after a quote is sent, and nothing else.
- A group's quotes must all be for the same client (a trigger checks it).
- The statement's scope lines always add up to the contracted value; when a
  revision reduced the scope it shows as "Reduced scope".
