# S21 — Bank balances follow recorded money; budgets "over" means overspent

**Depends on:** S12 (banking), S17 (budgets)
**State:** done

**Amends the plan:**

- **Recorded money moves the account.** S12 kept the register to what a bank
  statement reported, and migration 0031 said so: recording a payment against
  an account put nothing in it. With statement import not built, that left
  every balance frozen at its opening figure. Now a payment received into an
  account, or a confirmed expense paid from one, adds a line to that account
  (`fingerprint` `recorded:payment:<id>` / `recorded:expense:<id>`), already
  explained by the payment or expense. The line follows it: corrected, moved
  to another account, or removed with it. Money dated on or before the
  account's opening balance is left out -- that figure already includes it --
  as is money in another currency. A statement import, when it comes, can
  recognise these lines by their fingerprint.
- **Existing payments and expenses are placed once** by the worker's hourly
  pass (`placeRecordedMoney`), which also runs when the worker starts.
- **The opening balance can be corrected** until a period is reconciled
  against it (migration 0047). Moving it recomputes the balance at once;
  before, a corrected opening figure on an account without lines left the
  balance at the old one.
- **Adjust balance** (`bankAccount.adjustBalance`): set the account to what the
  bank shows on a day; the difference is a line, set aside with the reason
  (bank charges, interest, money not recorded here), and audited.
- **A budget is over only when more than it was spent.** Exactly 100% is a
  warning ("the whole budget is used"), and the alert email says so; before,
  spending exactly the budget was reported as over.
