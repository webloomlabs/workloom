# S20 — Closing the year: the books lock, a harder audit log, and the income tax pack

**Depends on:** S14 (financial year), S15 (expense categories and receipts), S18 (statement invoices), S19 (audit source)
**State:** done

**Amends the plan:**

- **The books lock** (`organization.books_locked_through`, `books.lock` /
  `books.unlock`, permission `books:lock`). Triggers on invoices, payments,
  allocations, expenses, and receipts refuse anything that would add, change,
  or remove money dated on or before the lock -- checking the old date and the
  new, so nothing moves in or out of the closed period. Still allowed: paying
  an old invoice with a payment received after the lock, attaching a missing
  receipt, rebilling an old expense. The refusal (SQLSTATE `WL001`) reaches the
  person as `books_locked`, in words.
- **The audit log, harder.** `TRUNCATE` revoked; whole-record `snapshot` on
  money records created or deleted; filters by person, action family, source,
  and dates; CSV export (`auditLog.export`); daily seals chained by SHA-256
  (`audit_seals`), checked by `auditSeal.verify`.
- **The income tax pack** (`tax_packs`, `taxPack.preflight|request|list|download`).
  The worker builds it as a zip -- written to a temporary file, receipts one at
  a time, then stored with the new `storage.putFile` -- holding:
  - `README.txt`: the headline figures, what to check, and every file's SHA-256;
  - `profit-and-loss.csv`, `revenue-schedule.csv`, `cash-receipts.csv`,
    `expense-schedule.csv`, `expenses-by-category.csv`, `missing-receipts.csv`;
  - `receipts/` named `EXP-0042_2025-06-14_hostinger.jpg`, and `receipts-index.csv`.

  Accrual basis, in the base currency, before tax. A statement's deduction
  lines mean a deposit and its final invoice count once. Expenses count by
  their category's treatment: deductible, not deductible (added back), or
  capital (listed for allowances). CSVs open in Excel (byte-order mark), and
  text from clients cannot run as a formula.

## Scope

In:
- `books.get|lock|unlock`, `taxPack.preflight|request|list|download`,
  `auditLog.export`, `auditSeal.verify`; `auditLog.list` filters.
- Worker: tax packs every 30 seconds; audit seals in the hourly pass.
- Web: Reports → Income tax pack (year picker, the year's figures, what to fix
  first with links, packs to download, close and reopen the books); Settings →
  Audit log filters, the source column, and Export CSV.

Out:
- VAT, WHT, and SSCL returns (income tax only, as agreed).
- A PDF of the profit and loss (the CSV and README carry it).
- Grouping the revenue report by financial year.
